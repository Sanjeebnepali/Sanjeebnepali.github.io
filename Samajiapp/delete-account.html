Delete your account

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

🗑️ You can delete your account yourself, inside the app, in under a minute. If you cannot use the app, you can ask us to do it by email (see below).

👤 What "your account" means in Shimeji Pet

Shimeji Pet has no account of its own. Signing in with Google is optional and is only needed to subscribe to a limited-edition Heroic pet. If you signed in, we hold two things online, in Google Firebase:

  • The sign-in record: your Google account ID, your Google display name and your email address.
  • Your owner record (only if you own a Heroic pet): your display name and the date your subscription is paid until, shown on that pet's page as one of its five owners.

📱 How to delete it in the app

  1. Open Shimeji Pet and go to Settings.
  2. In the Account card, tap Delete my account (it is shown while you are signed in).
  3. Read the message and tap Delete account.
  4. Google asks you to confirm. Choose the same Google account you signed in with.
  5. Done. You see "Your account was deleted."

✅ What is deleted

  • Your sign-in record (Google account ID, display name and email address) in Google Firebase.
  • Your owner record on every Heroic pet. Your name disappears from the owner lists at once and the spot becomes free for someone else.
  • This phone's note of which account held which spot.

📌 What is not affected

  • Your pets, level, progress and settings are stored only on your phone and are never linked to your Google account, so deleting the account does not change them. To erase them too, use Settings ➔ Account & Data Deletion ➔ Delete & Reset Local Data, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Google Play purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play, not by us.
⚠️ Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel your subscriptions in Google Play first, or you will keep being charged: open Google Play ➔ Payments & subscriptions ➔ Subscriptions. The app's delete screen has a button that opens it.

✉️ Cannot use the app? Ask us by email

If you no longer have the app or your phone, email us and we will delete your sign-in record and owner record for you:

  • Send an email to laligurasgardeningclub@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Shimeji account.
  • Write from the email address of the Google account you used to sign in, or tell us that address, so we can find the record.
  • We will confirm by reply once it is done, normally within 30 days.
